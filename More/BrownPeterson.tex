\begin{proposition}
The pullback $\Mfg\otimes \Z_{(p)}$ is represented by Hopf algebroid $(L_{(p)}, LB[u^{\pm}]_{(p)})$, and there exists isomorphism
\[
L_{(p)}\cong \Z_{(p)}[v_1, v_2,...], |v_i|=2(p^i-1)
\]and 
\[
LB[u^{\pm}]_{(p)}=L_{(p)}[t_1,...], |t_i|=2(p^i-1)
\]
\end{proposition}
