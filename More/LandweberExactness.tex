\begin{theorem}\cite{PreLandweber}
    A $MU_*$-module $M_*$ satisfying the following condition: there exists a prime $p$ and sequence of elements $v_i$ such that $(p,v_1,...)$ is a regular sequence in $M$. Then 
    \begin{align*}
        M_*(-)=MU_*(-)\otimes_{MU_*} M_*
    \end{align*}is a homology
\end{theorem}
    Any homology that comes from a Landweber module is called a Landweber homology
\begin{proposition}
    There does not exists phantom maps between Landweber homology.
\end{proposition}
