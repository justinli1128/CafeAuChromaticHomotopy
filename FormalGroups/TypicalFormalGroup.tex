\section{$p$-Typical Formal Group Law}\label{sec:formalgroups-typicalFormalGroup}

    We continue to work over $\Spec(\Z_{(p)})$ here.

    Let $F$ be a formal group law over a $p$-local algebra $A$. Suppose $n$ is a positive integer coprime to $p$, then the $n$-series $[n]_Ft:=t+_Ft+_F...+_F t$ ($n$ times) is an invertible formal power series, and we let $[1/n]_F t$ denote the inverse. Let $\zeta$ be a $n$th primitive root of unity, then there is power series
    \[
    f_n(t):=[\frac{1}{n}]_F (t+_F \zeta t+_F \zeta^2 t+_F...+_F \zeta^{n-1}t)
    \]This is actually a power series over $A$.
\begin{definition}
    A formal group law $F$ is \textbf{$p$-typical formal group law} if for all prime $q\neq p$, $f_q(t)=0$. A homomorphism of $p$-typical formal group law is just a morphism of formal group law.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}
    Suppose $F$ is a $p$-typical formal group law, then there exists element $v_i \in A$ with $p=v_0$ such that 
    \[
    [p]_F t=pt+_F v_1t^{p}+_F v_2t^{p^2}+_F...+_F v_nt^{p^n}+_F...
    \]
    Moreover, the element $v_i$ determines the $p$-typical formal group law uniquely.

    Notice, the height of $F$ is determined by the vanishing of $v_i$ entirely.
\end{remark}


\begin{theorem}
    There exists a formal group law $G$ over $V_p:=\Z_{(p)}[v_1,v_2,...]$, we refer to it as the \textbf{universal $p$-typical formal group law}, such that 
    \[
    [p]_Gt=pt+_Gv_1t^p+_Gv_2t^{p^2}+_G...
    \]such that every $p$-typical formal group law $F$ is determined uniquely by a map $\phi:V_p\to A$ such that $\phi$ factors through $V_p/I_n$ if $F$ is of height at least $n$, with the coefficient $v_i^F$ determined as $\phi(v_i)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}
    Every $p$-local formal group law is isomorphic to a $p$-typical formal group law.
\end{theorem}

The Hopf algebroid $(V_p, V_p\otimes_L LB[u^{\pm}]\otimes_L V_p)$, with the induced coaction determines a stack
\[
[\Spec(V_p)/\Spec(V_p\otimes_L LB[u^{\pm}]\otimes_L V_p)]\to \Mfg\otimes \Spec(\Z_{(p)})
\]
The following result shouldn't be too hard to believe 
\begin{proposition}
There is equivalence $[\Spec(V_p)/\Spec(V_p\otimes_L LB[u^{\pm}]\otimes_L V_p)]\simeq \Mfg\otimes \Spec(\Z_{(p)})$
\end{proposition}

    The closed immersion $\Spec(V_p/I_n)\to \Spec(V_p/I_n)$ with the induced Hopf algebroid determines the formal group laws of height at least $n$, and there is the following.
\begin{corollary}
    The inclusion $\Mfg^{\geq n}\to \Mfg\otimes \Spec(\Z_{(p)}$ is a closed immersion.
\end{corollary}
