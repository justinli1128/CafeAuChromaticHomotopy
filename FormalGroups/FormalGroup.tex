\section{Formal group}\label{sec:formalgroups-formalgroup}
% Write your notes here. Do not add a documentclass or document environment.
\begin{definition}
    Given a scheme $S$, a \textbf{formal group} over $S$ is a group object in the category of formal Lie varieties such that $\omega_e$ is locally free of rank $1$, and a \textbf{morphism of formal group} is a formal Lie variety morphism that preserves the group structure.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}
    Recall our identification of formal Lie varieties over affines implies a formal group over $\Spec(A)$ can be identified with a group structure on $\Spec(A[t])$, or equivalently a formal group law over $A$.
\end{remark}
