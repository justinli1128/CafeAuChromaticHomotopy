\begin{theorem}

    Let $M$ be a $L$-module, or equivalently a quasicoherent $\Spec(L)$-module $\tilde {M}$, then the functor
    \begin{align*}
        \Qcoh_{\Mfg}\xrightarrow{q^*}\Qcoh_{\Spec(L)}\xrightarrow{ -\otimes \tilde {M}}\Qcoh_{\Spec(L)}
    \end{align*}is exact if the following condition is satisfied: there exists a prime $p$, and a sequence of element $v_i\in M$ such that $(p,v_1,v_2,...)$ is a regular sequence in $M$.
